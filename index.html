How a report turns into a reward.

Nothing is paid for reporting. A spot has to be confirmed by three different phones (or one photo at a GPS position), then checked by the road department. Only when they schedule a fix does the first reporter get a small reward. Inventing a pothole earns nothing, because the department never finds one.

Steps 1 and 2 work in this prototype. Steps 3 to 5 need a link to the road department, which does not exist yet, so no reward is ever actually paid here. ₹50 is an example figure; the real amount would be the department's decision.

    Help boarding

    For wheelchair users, people who need a hand with the steps, and passengers with low vision.

    Third attempt, untested

    On 6 September 2026 we showed BusGo to nine bus drivers at a Hyderabad depot. Paid helpers were refused: extra cost, "a bit of commotion". The conductor was refused: "their hands are already full."

    So this version asks the other passengers on the bus first. A request shows up on their phones; whoever volunteers gets a coupon that makes their next ride cheaper. Only if nobody has volunteered by the time the bus is due is the conductor or driver asked, and they would be paid a stipend for it, because it is extra work.

    Nobody has tried this with drivers or passengers yet. In this prototype the request stays on this phone, so "other passengers" means other open tabs, and the fallback is a demo button.
