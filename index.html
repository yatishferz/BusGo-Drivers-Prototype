    Help boarding

    For wheelchair users, people who need help with the steps, and passengers with low vision.

    Open problem — not solved

    On 6 Sep 2026 we showed BusGo to nine bus drivers at a Hyderabad depot. One objected to bringing third-party helpers on board: extra cost, and it "would cause a bit of commotion". We proposed using the conductor instead — already on the bus, a small monthly stipend, optional tips. They refused that too: "their hands are already full."

    We don't have an answer yet. We kept this page because the passengers who need it are real. Your request is saved on this phone only. Nobody is dispatched. Who actually helps you board is a staffing and funding question, not something this app has solved.
